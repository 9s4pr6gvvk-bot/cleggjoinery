Why Choose Clegg Joinery

The same quality,
at commercial scale.

Commercial clients get exactly what domestic clients get, a craftsman who cares about the work and won't cut corners. We understand that commercial projects have tighter deadlines, stricter specifications and less tolerance for disruption, and we plan accordingly.

Whether it's a single fit-out or an ongoing commercial relationship, we're reliable, professional and easy to work with from first contact to final sign-off.

  • Offices, retail units, hospitality and public spaces
  • Bespoke counters, reception desks and display units
  • Fitted storage and commercial cabinetry
  • Internal doors, partitions and structural timber work
  • Out-of-hours working available to minimise disruption
  • Fully insured with public liability cover
