Why This Combination Works

The smart way to get a beautiful kitchen.

Plykea make handcrafted birch plywood door fronts and countertops designed to fit IKEA Sektion and Pax cabinets, giving you the warm, natural character of real exposed plywood at a fraction of the cost of a fully bespoke kitchen. Clegg Joinery handles the full installation, so everything fits and functions perfectly from day one.

You get the design freedom of a bespoke kitchen at a sensible price, fitted by an experienced joiner who takes pride in every detail.

  • Over 100 plywood materials and finishes to choose from
  • FSC-certified birch plywood core as standard
  • CNC-cut to precision, hand-finished in the Plykea workshop
  • Fits IKEA Sektion and Pax cabinets using standard fixings
  • Non-standard sizes and custom cover panels available
  • Full supply and fit by Clegg Joinery from start to finish
