Why Choose Clegg Joinery

A full refurb without the headache.

Most refurbishment projects fall apart in the coordination . Different tradespeople, clashing schedules and nobody taking overall responsibility for how the job looks at the end. We offer a different approach: one point of contact, one standard of workmanship, and a genuine commitment to seeing the whole project through.

Whether it's a single room or a whole house, we manage the joinery scope entirely . Where other trades are needed, we'll work alongside them or help you coordinate. The result is a home that feels considered and finished, not just repaired.

  • Full property and room-by-room refurbishments
  • Buy-to-let and rental property renovations
  • Period property and character home restoration
  • All joinery trades under one roof covering kitchens, bathrooms, floors, doors and more
  • Competitive pricing with a detailed scope of works
  • Reliable scheduling . We turn up when we say we will

Scope of Work

Everything under one roof

A full refurb draws on every area of our expertise all delivered to the same standard.

Kitchens

Bespoke fitted kitchens or Plykea installations Fully designed and fitted to your specification.

Bathrooms

Vanity units, fitted storage, panelling and complete bathroom joinery, transforming functional rooms into finished spaces.

Flooring

Doors & Windows
