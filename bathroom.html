Why Choose Clegg Joinery

The joinery makes the bathroom.

A bathroom is only as good as the craftsmanship behind it. We handle all the joinery elements that transform a functional room into something special, including bespoke vanity units, fitted storage, timber panelling, bath surrounds and more.

We work alongside your plumber and tiler or manage the full fit-out ourselves. Either way, the finish is immaculate.

  • Bespoke vanity units and under-sink storage
  • Fitted bathroom furniture and wall cabinets
  • Timber bath panels and surrounds
  • Tongue & groove and shiplap wall panelling
  • Mirror cabinets and bespoke shelving
  • Full bathroom fit-outs or joinery-only service
