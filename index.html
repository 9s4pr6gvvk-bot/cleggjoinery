About Clegg Joinery

Traditional craftsmanship,
modern results.

A trusted, family-run joinery business based in Slaithwaite, delivering outstanding bespoke woodwork for domestic and commercial clients throughout Huddersfield and the Colne Valley. With over 20 years' experience, every project is completed with the same pride, precision and passion for the craft.

  • All joinery designed and made to your exact specification
  • Domestic and commercial work across Huddersfield and surrounding areas
  • Reliable, punctual and professional on every job
  • Clean and respectful of your home or business
  • Competitive pricing with no hidden costs
